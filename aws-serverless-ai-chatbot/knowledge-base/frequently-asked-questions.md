# Frequently Asked Questions

## Do I need prior AWS experience?
No prior experience is required for the AWS Cloud Practitioner Foundations course. Other courses have prerequisites described in the course catalog.

## Do you offer virtual training?
Yes. The fictional academy offers virtual training and agreed corporate sessions.

## Is an AWS certification exam included?
No. Exam registration is separate from training unless a representative explicitly confirms otherwise.

## Can I pay through the chatbot?
No. Never share payment credentials with the chatbot. Request verified instructions from a representative.

## How much does training cost?
The demo does not provide fees. Contact a representative for current pricing.

## How can I register?
Ask a representative for the current schedule and enrollment form.
